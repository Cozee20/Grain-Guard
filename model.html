GrainGuard

Smart grain storage bin - 3D model (drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom)

Parts

  1. Bin body 80 x 80 x 100 cm
  2. Legs (4) 20 cm high
  3. Loading hatch 40 x 40 cm
  4. Perforated false floor + 10 cm air plenum
  5. Aeration fan 12 V DC, back wall
  6. Exhaust vent with insect mesh
  7. Sensor probe with 3 temp/RH sensors
  8. Control box display, LEDs, buzzer
  9. Discharge gate 20 x 15 cm
  10. Level window

Fan switches on when humidity is above 70 % RH or temperature above 30 C. Buzzer sounds above 40 C.

Use Cutaway to see the probe, sensors, grain and the air plenum.
